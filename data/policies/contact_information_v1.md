# Contact Information Update Policy

Policy ID: HA-CONTACT
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 9.1 Contact Updates

Applicants may request changes to their mailing address, telephone number, or email address.

## 9.2 Identity Confirmation

Contact information changes require confirmation that the requested change belongs to the correct case.

## 9.3 Address Changes

Address changes must be recorded in the case history.

A contact-information update does not by itself change eligibility or benefit amount.
