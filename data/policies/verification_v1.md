# Housing Assistance Verification Policy

Policy ID: HA-VER
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 7.1 Required Verification

Applicants may be required to provide income, residency, or household verification.

## 7.2 Submitted Documents

A document that has been uploaded but has not yet been reviewed must be treated as pending review rather than missing.

Adverse action should not be taken solely because a submitted document remains unreviewed.

## 7.3 Response Period

When additional verification is requested, the applicant must be given ten business days to respond before case closure may be considered.

## 7.4 Case Closure

Case closure related to missing verification requires human approval.

Before closure, CivicFlow must confirm that the required response period has elapsed and that no relevant submitted document remains unreviewed.
