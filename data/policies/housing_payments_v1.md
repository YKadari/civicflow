# Housing Assistance Payment Policy

Policy ID: HA-PAY
Version: 1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 4.1 Payment Schedule

Housing assistance payments are scheduled for the first business day of each month.

## 4.2 Missing Payments

If a scheduled payment has not been received within five business days of the scheduled payment date, a payment-status investigation may be opened.

Before opening an investigation, the case must be active and required verification documents must not be expired.

## 4.3 Held Payments

A payment may be placed on hold when required eligibility or income verification is incomplete or awaiting review.

A held payment must not automatically result in case closure.

## 4.4 Payment Investigation

A payment investigation should document the affected payment, the payment status, and any relevant case verification information.
